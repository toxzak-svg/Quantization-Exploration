KEY FINDINGS:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SVD Sub1Bit with 90% threshold FAILS because:
   - Rank is too high (almost no dimensionality reduction)
   - Ternary quantization on near-full-rank matrices adds noise
   - The reported 18x compression is misleading - actual rank ~95% of max

2. Ternary Aggressive achieves best balance:
   - 10x compression (good)
   - ~66 estimated PPL (decent, assuming base Gemma 4 E2B PPL ~5)
   - Direct quantization preserves quality better than SVD at high ranks

3. Magnitude Quant (4-bit/2-bit):
   - 7.5x compression (lower)
   - ~128 estimated PPL (worse than ternary)
   - Per-channel scales help but quantization noise still significant

RECOMMENDATIONS:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. For BEST compression with acceptable quality:
   - Use Ternary Aggressive approach (1-2 bits, importance weighted)
   - Target: ~1.5-2.0 bpw, 8-10x compression
   - Expected perplexity: 50-100 on WikiText-2

2. For BETTER quality with moderate compression:
   - Use Magnitude Quant with 4-bit for early layers, 2-bit for later
   - Target: ~2-3 bpw, 5-8x compression
   - Expected perplexity: 30-60 on WikiText-2

3. For SVD-based approach to work:
   - MUST use lower energy threshold (50-70%)
   - Or use learned transforms to make weights more compressible
   - The train_transform.py approach is the right direction
